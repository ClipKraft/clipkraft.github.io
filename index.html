Questions

The short answers.

Is it free?

The studio is free. The AI features run on your own free Puter account, which comes with a monthly allowance. Heavy use, especially AI video clips, can use it up; you can top up your account or continue next month.

Do I need an account?

You'll be asked to sign in to Puter (or continue as a guest) the first time you use an AI feature. Your projects themselves are saved in your browser.

Where is my video rendered?

On your own device, in the browser. Nothing has to be uploaded to edit or export, which is why it works fast on a decent laptop or phone.

Which languages are supported?

Scripts and voiceovers work in English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and more.

Can I use my own images, clips, voice or music?

Yes. Any scene can take your own image or video, any scene's voiceover can be your own recording, and you can add a music track you have the rights to.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The studio is built mobile-first and can be installed to your home screen. Long 1080p exports are quicker on a laptop.
